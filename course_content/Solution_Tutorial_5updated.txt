(b) Is the diamond thermodynamically stable relative to graphite at 298 K?

       On basis of result in Q2(a), there is a net increase in Gibbs free energy (DG is
       positive) when graphite transforms to diamond. Hence this reaction will not be
       spontaneous, and diamond is not thermodynamically stable relative to graphite.
       It is metastable.

(e) In industrial practice, diamond is synthesized from graphite to diamond at high
       pressure and temperature. Qn 3d explained the high pressure. Explain why the
       need for high temperature ­ qualitatively.

At this pressure the system must also be at approximately 1000 K in order for
the conversion rate to be high enough for the process to be practical.
Thermodynamics tells us whether or not something can occur, and allows us to
calculate the conditions for making something happen, but it says absolutely
nothing at all about how fast or slow that process will be.
